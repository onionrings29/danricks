## SECTION 8: Upload Instructions

I've created folders inside **`contents/`** for all the files we need. Here's where to put everything:

### Folder Structure:
```
contents/
├── logo/            ← Your logo file(s) here (PNG or SVG preferred, transparent background is best)
├── hero-slideshow/  ← 3-5 of your BEST event photos for the homepage slideshow
├── gallery/         ← All your gallery photos — organize in subfolders by event if you can!
│   ├── event-name-1/
│   ├── event-name-2/
│   └── ...
├── about/           ← Photo of you (if you want one on the About page)
├── services/        ← Photos representing each package (e.g., booth setup, sample outputs per tier)
└── testimonials/    ← Screenshots of reviews/testimonials (if any)
```

### What to upload:

| Folder | What goes here | How many |
|--------|---------------|----------|
| `logo/` | Your logo (PNG/SVG, transparent background if possible) | 1-2 files |
| `hero-slideshow/` | Your absolute BEST photos — these are the first thing visitors see | 3-5 photos |
| `gallery/` | All event photos you want on the gallery page. Create a subfolder per event if you can! (e.g., `gallery/sarah-debut/`, `gallery/xyz-corporate/`) | As many as you want |
| `about/` | A photo of you for the About page (optional) | 1 photo |
| `services/` | Photos showing your booth setup, sample outputs, or anything that represents each package tier | 2-5 photos |
| `testimonials/` | Screenshots of customer reviews from Instagram, Google, Facebook, etc. | As many as you have |

### Photo tips:
- **Landscape orientation** (horizontal) works best for hero/banner photos
- **High resolution** is better — we'll resize as needed
- If photos are too large to upload, let me know and we'll figure out another way!
